## Changelog

### 1.1.0 (2026-10-28)

- Added `pricing-models`: price determinants, supply and demand, elasticity, all nine pricing models with advantages and disadvantages, combining models, pricing psychology, and price testing.
- Added `financials`: income statement, cash flow statement, balance sheet, forecasting, break-even, and using financials to decide.
- Added pointers to `pricing-models` and `financials` across the existing skills; `business-model` also gained growth mindset and openness to pivoting.

### 1.0.0 (2026-10-17)

- Added 11 entrepreneurship skills: `business-model`, `creative-ideation`, `customer-ecosystem`, `customer-end-user-profile-and-persona`, `customer-interview`, `customer-segmentation`, `customer-validation`, `market-research`, `opportunity-id`, `problem-statement`, and `value-proposition`.
- Removed the `alpha` and `beta` stub skills.
- Updated plugin and marketplace descriptions.

### 0.1.0 (2026-10-16)

- Initial release: plugin skeleton with `alpha` and `beta` stub skills.
